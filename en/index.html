Research example · 11 Nov 2026 · Nigeria → Spain
QuestionA Nigerian family is moving to Spain, while the head of the family keeps receiving dividends from his Nigerian company. Is there a double taxation treaty between the two countries, and where is the tax due in the first year?
In short

Yes: the Spain–Nigeria double taxation treaty was signed in 2009 and has been in force since 5 June 2015. After more than 183 days in Spain, the head of the family becomes a Spanish tax resident for the whole calendar year of the move, and Spain taxes his dividends at rates from 19% to 30%. Nigeria withholds no more than 10% at source, and that tax is deducted from the Spanish tax.

The treaty between the two countries

The double taxation treaty between Spain and Nigeria was signed in Abuja on 23 June 2009 and has been in force since 5 June 2015.

Primary source: BOE, Spain–Nigeria treaty, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

If both countries treat a person as resident, the treaty settles it step by step: a permanent home available to him, then the centre of vital interests, then habitual abode and nationality. So it matters whether the home in Nigeria stays available to the family or is let out.

Primary source: BOE, Spain–Nigeria treaty, art. 4.2, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Residence in the year of the move

More than 183 days in Spain in a calendar year make a person a Spanish tax resident; the tax period is the calendar year.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, arts. 9.1.a and 12, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Spanish law has no split year for a move: a tax period shorter than the calendar year exists only when the taxpayer dies. So Spain taxes the income of the whole year of the move, including the months before the move.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, art. 13, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

The law also presumes Spanish residence when the spouse and the dependent minor children live habitually in Spain; only evidence to the contrary can rebut this.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, art. 9.1, last paragraph, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Under Nigeria’s 2025 Act, in force since 1 January 2026, a person is resident if any one test is met: domicile, an available home, substantial economic and immediate family ties, or 183 days in 12 months. So a short stay in Nigeria does not by itself end Nigerian residence, and the conflict then goes to article 4.2 of the treaty.

Primary source: Nigeria Tax Act 2025, s. 201, definition of resident individual, nass.gov.ng, checked 12.11.2026

Dividends: tax in Nigeria

Nigeria withholds no more than 10% on dividends paid to an individual resident in Spain; the 7.5% rate applies only to a company holding at least 10%.

Primary source: BOE, Spain–Nigeria treaty, art. 10.2, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

The tax a Nigerian company withholds from a non-resident’s dividends and pays to the tax authority is his final Nigerian tax. The Act leaves the withholding rate itself to separate regulations on deduction of tax at source.

Primary source: Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, s. 51(1) and 51(2), nass.gov.ng, checked 12.11.2026

Dividends: tax in Spain

A Spanish resident pays personal income tax (IRPF) on all income, wherever it arises and wherever the payer is resident. So all the dividends are taxed, not only the part transferred to Spain.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, art. 2, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Dividends fall into the savings base, taxed in bands: 19% up to 6,000 euros, 21% up to 50,000, 23% up to 200,000, 27% up to 300,000 and 30% above that. These are the state and regional halves added together, 9.5–15% each.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, arts. 25.1.a, 46, 66 and 76, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

The treaty removes double taxation by a credit: Spain deducts the tax paid in Nigeria from its own tax, but no more than the part of the Spanish tax that falls on that income.

Primary source: BOE, Spain–Nigeria treaty, art. 24, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Under Spanish law the deduction is the lower of two amounts: the tax actually paid abroad, or the Spanish tax at the average effective rate on that income. The savings base rate starts at 19% and Nigerian withholding is at most 10%, so the difference is paid in Spain.

Primary source: BOE, Personal Income Tax Act 35/2006, art. 80.1, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

Reporting assets abroad

A Spanish resident reports every year on his accounts abroad, and separately on holdings in foreign companies and on homes abroad. There is no duty for a group whose total at 31 December does not exceed 50,000 euros.

Primary source: BOE, Royal Decree 1065/2007, arts. 42 bis, 42 ter and 54 bis, boe.es, checked 12.11.2026

This information is filed on Form 720; the Tax Agency page gives the filing period as 1 January to 31 March 2026.

Primary source: Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT), Form 720, filing periods, sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es, checked 12.11.2026
What is left to check
  • Dividends for the months before the move: if Nigeria treats him as resident for those months, how the treaty allocates them. A tax adviser in Spain answers this.
  • The Nigerian withholding rate under the regulations on deduction of tax at source: we did not open the official text of those regulations. The company’s accounts department issues the rate and the withholding certificate needed for the Spanish credit; in case of doubt, the Nigeria Revenue Service.
  • Whether he keeps Nigerian residence through domicile and his ties to the company, and which residence certificate is needed to apply the treaty. A Nigerian tax adviser and the Nigeria Revenue Service answer this, and in Spain the AEAT.
  • Not checked: the controlled foreign company rules (art. 91 of the Personal Income Tax Act) for the holding in the Nigerian company, regional wealth tax, and penalties related to Form 720. A tax adviser in Spain answers this.

Research example · 11 Nov 2026Singapore → Dubai: a family and its own company
QuestionThe owner of a small trading company in Singapore is moving to Dubai with his family. What changes for his tax residence, which visa suits him and his family, and what should he do with the company: move it or open a new one in the UAE?
In short

If you spend 183 days or more a year in the UAE, the UAE treats you as a tax resident; for Singapore, as a citizen, what matters is not a day count but where you normally reside. A visa for you and your family runs through your own UAE company: the 5-year Green visa for investors and partners lets you sponsor your spouse and children. The Singapore company stays tax resident in Singapore while its strategic decisions are made there; if they are made from Dubai, it may become a UAE resident, so the plan to keep the Singapore company and open a new one in the UAE rests on a local director with real authority.

Personal tax residence

The UAE treats a person as tax resident if he has been physically present in the country for 183 days or more within 12 consecutive months, or if his usual place of residence and the centre of his financial and personal interests are in the UAE.

Primary source: UAE Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022, art. 4.1–4.2, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

A UAE residence permit holder needs only 90 days within 12 consecutive months if he has a permanent home, a job or a business in the UAE.

Primary source: UAE Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022, art. 4.3, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

A Singapore citizen remains tax resident if he normally resides in Singapore, apart from temporary absences; IRAS applies the 183-day rule to foreigners. In your case what counts is where you normally live after the move, not only the number of days.

Primary source: IRAS, “Working out my tax residency” page, iras.gov.sg, checked 12.11.2026

Visas for you and your family

The Green visa is issued to investors and business partners in the UAE for 5 years and can be renewed. You must show proof of an investment or a stake in a UAE business and obtain the licences; the holder can sponsor a spouse and children.

Primary source: ICP, Green Residence guide, icp.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

The 10-year Golden visa for investors: among its conditions ICP lists company capital or a partner’s share in the UAE of at least AED 2,000,000 from own funds, not a loan, and a company paying at least AED 250,000 in taxes a year.

Primary source: ICP, Golden Residency guide, investors section, icp.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

An expatriate resident can sponsor a spouse, unmarried daughters and sons under 25. For employees’ families, the u.ae portal sets a minimum sponsor salary of AED 4,000 a month, or AED 3,000 plus accommodation.

Primary source: UAE government portal u.ae, “Residence visa for families of employees”, u.ae, checked 12.11.2026

If the sponsor is a partner in a company, GDRFA Dubai requires his share under the partnership agreement to be at least AED 48,000.

Primary source: GDRFA Dubai, “Entry visa for residence without work, family sponsorship” service, gdrfad.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

The Singapore company: management and the owner’s income

A company is tax resident in Singapore when its control and management, meaning its strategic decisions, is exercised in Singapore; usually that is where the board of directors meets.

Primary source: IRAS, “Tax residency of a company / Certificate of Residence”, iras.gov.sg, checked 12.11.2026

A director may leave office only if the company still has at least one director who is ordinarily resident in Singapore. So the local director has to be appointed before you step down.

Primary source: ACRA, “When a director can or must leave office”, acra.gov.sg, checked 12.11.2026

If you stay on as director and become a non-resident, Singapore withholds 24% of your director’s fees.

Primary source: IRAS, types of payment and withholding tax rates, iras.gov.sg, checked 12.11.2026

Dividends paid by a Singapore resident company under the one-tier system are not taxed in the shareholder’s hands. This holds while the company itself stays resident in Singapore.

Primary source: IRAS, dividends page, iras.gov.sg, checked 12.11.2026

The UAE company: tax and place of management

A company incorporated outside the UAE but effectively managed and controlled in the UAE is a UAE resident person for corporate tax.

Primary source: UAE Federal Tax Authority, tax residency guide TPGTR1, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

UAE corporate tax: 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on the part above that amount.

Primary source: UAE Cabinet Decision No. 116 of 2022, art. 2–3, mof.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

A free zone company with Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) status pays 0% on qualifying income and 9% on the rest, without the untaxed first AED 375,000.

Primary source: UAE Federal Tax Authority, Free Zone Persons guide CTGFZP1, section 3.3, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026
What is left to check
  • Whether the Singapore company can transfer its registration to the UAE and keep its legal identity: we found no official text on such a transfer; ACRA and the registrar of the chosen UAE zone can answer.
  • What the licence and the family visas will cost in total: there is no official total; you need the current tariff of the chosen free zone or of Dubai’s Department of Economy and Tourism.
  • Whether your trading counts as a qualifying free zone activity and which income would be non-qualifying: a tax adviser decides this under the UAE Federal Tax Authority rules.
  • How the Singapore–UAE double taxation agreement settles dual residence and taxes director’s fees: check the treaty text on the IRAS website with a tax adviser.

Research example · 11 Nov 2026Vietnam → Argentina: a stake in a company
QuestionAn entrepreneur from Vietnam is moving to Argentina and keeping a stake in a Vietnamese company. Is there a double taxation treaty between Vietnam and Argentina, and if not, how does each country’s domestic law avoid double tax on dividends?
In short

There is no double tax treaty in force between Vietnam and Argentina: Argentina lists it as negotiated but not signed. Vietnam takes 5% of the dividends of a Vietnamese company, and once residence begins Argentina taxes them on its own scale and credits the Vietnamese tax up to the Argentine tax. On a temporary residence permit, Argentine tax residence begins only after 12 months.

Is there a treaty

There is no double tax treaty in force between Argentina and Vietnam. Argentina lists it among treaties negotiated and pending signature.

Primary source: Argentine Ministry of Economy, list of treaties negotiated and pending signature, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

How Vietnam taxes the dividends

A profit share a member receives from a Vietnamese limited liability company is capital investment income. For a Vietnamese resident it is taxed at 5% on each payment.

Primary source: Vietnam Government Portal, Decree 253/2026/ND-CP, arts. 9 and 52, chinhphu.vn, checked 12.11.2026

For a non-resident of Vietnam the rate is the same: 5% of the full capital investment income from Vietnamese entities. A change of residence therefore does not change the Vietnamese tax on these dividends.

Primary source: Vietnam Personal Income Tax Law, consolidated text 112/VBHN-VPQH, art. 22, chinhphu.vn, checked 12.11.2026

A person who keeps a permanent place of residence in Vietnam stays a Vietnamese resident even with fewer than 183 days there, unless they prove residence in another country with a certificate of residence.

Primary source: Government of Vietnam e-newspaper, text of art. 4 of Decree 253/2026/ND-CP, baochinhphu.vn, checked 12.11.2026

When Argentine tax residence begins

A foreign national on a temporary residence permit becomes an Argentine tax resident only after 12 months of stay; with a permanent permit, on obtaining it.

Primary source: ARCA, income tax: who is a resident, arca.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

Absences of up to 90 days in total, consecutive or not, within each 12-month period do not break that term.

Primary source: Decree 862/2019 (Income Tax Law regulations), art. 281, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

Resident status takes effect from the first day of the month after the month in which the 12 months end or the permanent permit is obtained.

Primary source: Income Tax Law (text of Decree 824/2019), art. 116, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

For countries without a treaty, ARCA issues an Argentine tax residence certificate, but only for periods in which the person was already an Argentine resident. This certificate is the proof of residence for Vietnam.

Primary source: ARCA, General Resolution 5572/2024, arts. 2 and 3, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

How Argentina taxes the dividends and credits the Vietnamese tax

An Argentine resident is taxed on income from Argentina and abroad; a non-resident only on Argentine-source income. Before residence begins, the Vietnamese dividends are therefore not taxed in Argentina.

Primary source: ARCA, income tax: basic concepts, arca.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

Dividends from a foreign company are foreign-source income taxed in full. The tax is computed with the general progressive scale of art. 94: marginal rates from 5% to 35%.

Primary source: Income Tax Law (text of Decree 824/2019), arts. 137, 138, 164 and 94, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

The Vietnamese tax actually paid is deducted from the Argentine tax on the foreign income, up to the amount of that Argentine tax. Any unused balance carries forward for the next 5 years.

Primary source: Income Tax Law (text of Decree 824/2019), arts. 165 and 175, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

With a direct holding of at least 25%, the matching share of the profit tax paid by the Vietnamese company itself can also be credited. This requires a document from the tax authority showing payment.

Primary source: Decree 862/2019 (Income Tax Law regulations), arts. 305 and 306, argentina.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

Argentine personal assets tax

Since 2019 the personal assets tax (Bienes Personales) follows tax residence, including assets abroad. The rate above the tax-free threshold is 0.50–0.75% for 2026 and 0.25% for 2027.

Primary source: Law 23,966, Title VI, arts. 17 and 25 (ARCA consolidated text), biblioteca.afip.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026

Taxable assets include shares and quotas in companies domiciled abroad, which covers the stake in the Vietnamese company.

Primary source: ARCA, personal assets tax: assets covered, arca.gob.ar, checked 12.11.2026
What is left to check
  • The official list of Vietnam’s tax treaties on the websites of Vietnam’s Ministry of Finance and tax administration could not be opened; the Vietnamese side of the talks is a question for Vietnam’s tax administration (Cuc Thue).
  • No official date has been announced for signature or entry into force; watch the Argentine Ministry of Economy list.
  • ARCA has not yet published the art. 94 bracket amounts or the personal assets tax-free threshold for 2027; an exact figure needs an Argentine tax accountant (contador) once they are published.
  • Which document on the 5% withholding the Vietnamese company will issue and whether ARCA accepts it for the credit, and whether Argentine controlled foreign company rules apply, are questions for tax advisers in both countries.

Research example · 11 Nov 2026Kazakhstan · UAE · Georgia: a flat and a rental
QuestionA citizen of Kazakhstan with a UAE residence visa is disposing of a flat in Almaty and earning rental income in Georgia. Where and how is this income taxed, which double taxation treaties apply, and does UAE residence give a right to their benefits?
In short

Georgia taxes the rent from Georgia: 5% of income from housing let for residential use with no deductions; the standard rate is 20%. Tax on disposing of the Almaty flat depends on residence in Kazakhstan, decided by 183 days or the centre of vital interests: housing bought before 2026 is exempt after a year of ownership, while a non-resident pays 15% of the positive difference. A UAE visa does not unlock the Georgia–UAE treaty, and Kazakhstan’s treaties with Georgia and with the UAE leave the right to tax income from housing with the country where it is located.

Tax residence in Kazakhstan

Either of two grounds makes a person a tax resident of Kazakhstan: permanent stay or a centre of vital interests. Permanent stay means at least 183 calendar days, counting days of arrival and departure, in any consecutive 12-month period ending in the tax period.

Primary source: Tax Code of Kazakhstan, art. 222(1)–(2), adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

The centre of vital interests is in Kazakhstan when three conditions are met together: Kazakh citizenship, a residence permit or a residence card; a spouse or close relatives live in Kazakhstan, a condition that counts only if there are any; and housing in Kazakhstan, owned or held by the person, the spouse or close relatives, available at any time to live in. So a person can remain resident even after a short stay: the facts about family and housing decide it.

Primary source: Tax Code of Kazakhstan, art. 222(3), adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

The Almaty flat

Housing in Kazakhstan bought before 1 January 2026 can be disposed of tax-free after 1 year of ownership; bought later, after 2 years.

Primary source: State Revenue Committee, Pavlodar region department, 19.08.2026, gov.kz, checked 12.11.2026

For a non-resident of Kazakhstan, the gain on disposing of the flat is taxed at 15% of the positive difference between the disposal value and the acquisition cost. The list of a non-resident’s exempt income (art. 681) has no exemption for the holding period of housing.

Primary source: Tax Code of Kazakhstan, art. 682(1)(5), art. 687(1), adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

If the buyer is not a tax agent, the non-resident files a return by 1 July of the following year and pays the tax within 10 calendar days after that deadline. If the buyer is a tax agent, the person disposing of the flat gives the buyer a document showing the acquisition cost; otherwise tax is withheld on the full disposal value.

Primary source: Tax Code of Kazakhstan, arts. 695(3), 697(1), 687(8), adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

The rent in Georgia

Georgian income from housing let for residential use is taxed at 5% with no deductions; the standard rate is 20%.

Primary source: Tax Code of Georgia, art. 81(1)–(2), matsne.gov.ge, checked 12.11.2026

A Georgian resident is someone who stayed there 183 days or more in any continuous 12-month period, so on short visits the person stays a non-resident of Georgia. A non-resident files a return by 1 April of the following year if the tax on the income was not withheld at source.

Primary source: Tax Code of Georgia, art. 34(2), art. 153(1)(c), matsne.gov.ge, checked 12.11.2026

Which treaties apply

The Ministry of Finance of Georgia lists its double tax treaties with Kazakhstan (since 05.07.2000) and with the UAE (since 28.04.2011) as in force.

Primary source: Ministry of Finance of Georgia, list of treaties, mof.ge, checked 12.11.2026

Under the Kazakhstan–Georgia treaty, income from a flat, house or land in Georgia, including rent, may be taxed in Georgia. If the person is a resident of Kazakhstan, Kazakhstan deducts the Georgian tax from its own, up to the Kazakh tax on that income.

Primary source: Kazakhstan–Georgia treaty, art. 6(1) and (3), art. 24, adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

The Kazakhstan–UAE treaty leaves Kazakhstan the right to tax gains from disposing of a flat, house or land located in Kazakhstan, so it does not exempt the Almaty flat.

Primary source: Kazakhstan–UAE treaty, art. 13(1), adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026

A UAE visa does not unlock the Georgia–UAE treaty: for individuals, a UAE resident under it means a UAE national.

Primary source: Ministry of Finance of Georgia, Georgia–UAE treaty, art. 5(1)(b)(i), mof.ge, checked 12.11.2026

UAE residence and treaty benefits

An individual becomes a UAE tax resident, among other grounds, by being present in the country for 183 days or more within 12 consecutive months, or for 90 days or more with a residence permit and either a permanent place of residence or employment in the UAE. Where an international agreement sets its own residence conditions, those apply for that agreement.

Primary source: UAE Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022, arts. 4 and 6, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

The UAE Federal Tax Authority issues a Tax Residency Certificate for a current or past period of up to 12 months, never for a future period. A certificate for applying tax treaties is issued under Ministerial Decision No. 247 of 2023.

Primary source: UAE Federal Tax Authority, tax certificates page, tax.gov.ae, checked 12.11.2026

Kazakhstan applies a treaty exemption to a non-resident’s income from a person who is not a tax agent only if, on the date the return is filed, the person holds a residence document. Without it the tax is paid and can be reclaimed later.

Primary source: Tax Code of Kazakhstan, art. 714, adilet.zan.kz, checked 12.11.2026
What is left to check
  • Whether Kazakhstan treats a person without UAE nationality as a UAE resident for the Kazakhstan–UAE treaty, and how dual residence is resolved under art. 4(2) of that treaty: no official position of the State Revenue Committee was found. The Committee answers a written request; a tax adviser can also help.
  • Whether a private buyer is a tax agent when buying the flat from a non-resident, that is, who withholds and pays the tax: the Code speaks of a “buyer who is a tax agent”, but no direct answer for individuals was found. The State Revenue Committee answers this.
  • The register of residential landlords and the relief for letting to legal persons are set by the Minister of Finance of Georgia (art. 81(5)); that order was not opened, and we found no payment deadline in the Code for a non-resident’s annual tax. The Revenue Service of Georgia answers this.
  • The requirements of UAE Ministerial Decision No. 247 of 2023 for a treaty certificate, and how Kazakhstan accepts it, were not checked; if the person is a resident of Kazakhstan, the Kazakh tax on the Georgian rent depends on other income. The FTA, the State Revenue Committee and a tax adviser answer this.

Research example · 11 Nov 2026Thailand: importing goods
QuestionI want to import small batches of goods into Thailand. Which duties and taxes are paid on import, does the importer need a licence, and is there a value threshold for simplified clearance?
In short

Importing into Thailand means import duty at the rate for the specific product and 7% VAT on the CIF value plus duty; an importer pays VAT even without VAT registration. The official list of clearance documents has no general “importer licence”: the company registers with the Customs Department, and permits are needed for controlled goods, such as phone chargers under a mandatory TISI standard. Since 1 January 2026 there is in effect no duty-free threshold (1 baht), and simplified clearance of express and postal shipments covers consignments up to 40,000 baht FOB.

Import duty

Duty equals the CIF customs value (cost of goods, insurance and freight) multiplied by the duty rate for that product.

Primary source: Thai Customs Department, FAQ on duty calculation, customs.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

A preferential free-trade rate, for example for goods from China, applies only when a certificate of origin is submitted.

Primary source: Thailand National Trade Repository (Department of Trade Negotiations), import procedures, thailandntr.com, checked 12.11.2026

The customs fee for lodging a declaration is 200 baht per document, and 100 baht for a request to enter the data into the electronic system.

Primary source: Ministerial Regulation on customs fees B.E. 2560 (2017), cl. 1(3), customs.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

Import VAT and VAT registration

The 7% VAT rate, local tax included, has been extended to 30 September 2027.

Primary source: Revenue Department, press release 18/2569 on the VAT rate, rd.go.th, checked 11.11.2026

Import VAT is paid by the importer even if it is not registered for VAT; the Customs Department collects it at the time of import.

Primary source: Revenue Department, Value Added Tax page, rd.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

The import VAT base is the CIF value plus import duty, excise tax and other fees where they apply.

Primary source: Thai Revenue Code, section 79/2, rd.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

The Customs Department receipt for import VAT is deemed a tax invoice, so a registered VAT payer can treat that VAT as input tax.

Primary source: Revenue Department Order Por. 86/2542, clause 20, rd.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

VAT registration is mandatory once turnover exceeds 1.8 million baht a year; the application is due within 30 days.

Primary source: Revenue Department, VAT registration, rd.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

Importer registration and permits

To clear goods through the electronic customs system, a company must register with the Customs Department; there are three channels for this, including filing in person.

Primary source: Customs Department, “Importer/exporter registration” page, customs.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

The official list of documents for duty payment names an import licence only for controlled goods, listed by the Customs Department; the list has no separate general importer licence.

Primary source: Thailand National Trade Repository, import procedures, thailandntr.com, checked 12.11.2026

Power supplies for mobile phones and tablets are on the list of products that by law must conform to standard TIS 62368 part 1-2563.

Primary source: TISI, database of products under mandatory standards, tisi.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

Threshold for simplified clearance

Since 1 January 2026, only goods with a customs value of no more than 1 baht are exempt from Thai import duty; the threshold used to be 1,500 baht.

Primary source: Customs Department, Notification No. 219/2568 (Royal Gazette, 11 Dec 2025), ratchakitcha.soc.go.th, checked 11.11.2026

Since 1 January 2026, an express shipment worth no more than 40,000 baht FOB per House Air Waybill is cleared in simplified category 3: the customs system releases it on the “green line” or sends it for inspection.

Primary source: Customs Department, Notification No. 230/2568 on express consignments, cl. 2, customs.go.th, checked 12.11.2026

An international postal item worth no more than 40,000 baht FOB is cleared on the postal notice with duty paid on the spot, including for a company; above that, a full import declaration is required.

Primary source: Customs Department, postal clearance work manual, ed. 5 of 15 May 2026, sec. 6.4.6–6.4.7, customs.go.th, checked 12.11.2026
What is left to check
  • The duty rate for each charger, case and headphone model is not given here: it is found by tariff code in the Customs Department’s Integrated Tariff Database or through a customs broker.
  • Whether Bluetooth headphones need an NBTC procedure (SDoC declaration or certification): the NBTC page did not open for checking; ask NBTC for the specific model.
  • How and when an importer obtains a TISI licence for chargers (before release from customs or later): the standards act text did not open; ask TISI.
  • Whether excise tax applies to specific goods, for example batteries: ask the Excise Department.
