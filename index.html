Synapse

Grounded answers for real-estate and relocation decisions

Buying, leasing or relocating across borders means living with questions that have no single reliable answer online: which permit applies, what a clause really allows, what a number was on a given date. Synapse takes such a question and returns a decision-ready answer: each claim with the source it rests on and a confidence level, instead of a stream of links.

Under the hood it is a multi-model pipeline: several models work the question independently, their output is checked against primary sources (laws, registries, official rates), and anything that cannot be backed by a source is marked as such rather than hidden. Live numbers such as exchange rates come from deterministic public data, never from a model's memory.

Synapse is decision support for people and teams who work with property and relocation. It does not replace a lawyer or a notary.
